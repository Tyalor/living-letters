Living Letters

A small experiment in three steps: cellular automata that run free for a while, then drift into a word, then let go of it.

01Conway's Game of LifeThe classic. Draw cells, drop in gliders and guns, watch it go. 02Life forms a wordLife runs on its own for a few seconds, then the cells are nudged, patch by patch, into hand-lettered text. Never perfectly. 03LeniaThe continuous version. Orbium and friends glide around, then the whole field is pulled into the word, holds it, breathing, and dissolves it again.
